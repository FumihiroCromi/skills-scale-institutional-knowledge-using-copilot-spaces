# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA / Testing

### Role Summary
QA professionals validate that solutions meet quality standards and acceptance criteria. They work across the team to ensure testing is comprehensive and that products reach users with confidence.

### Responsibilities
- Design and execute test plans and acceptance criteria validation
- Identify and document quality issues and edge cases
- Collaborate with developers on test coverage and automation
- Support release readiness and smoke testing
- Help teams understand quality risks and dependencies

### Goals
- Ensure products meet acceptance criteria and quality standards
- Reduce post-release defects and customer issues
- Maintain clear quality visibility across the team

### Typical Communication
- Test planning during sprint planning and review
- Issue reports and quality metrics
- Acceptance validation and release sign-off

---

## Scrum Master / Agile Coach

### Role Summary
The Scrum Master or Agile Coach helps the team work effectively, improve delivery flow, and remove impediments that slow progress. They support the team's agile practices while keeping work aligned to the project plan and team goals.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate sprint planning, standups, reviews, and retrospectives
- Remove or escalate blockers affecting team progress
- Coach the team on agile practices and continuous improvement
- Help maintain a healthy, collaborative working rhythm
- Work with the Project Manager and Product Manager to improve team flow and visibility

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Product Manager**: Align on prioritization, delivery flow, and dependencies
- **Project Manager**: Coordinate ceremonies, delivery tracking, and risk communication
- **Developers**: Coach agile practices, facilitate team problem-solving, and unblock friction
- **QA**: Coordinate test readiness and feedback loops within the sprint cadence
- **Stakeholders**: Provide transparency on team progress and delivery risks

### Goals
- Improve team effectiveness and sustainability
- Reduce blockers and workflow friction
- Increase predictability and team alignment

### Typical Communication
- Sprint ceremonies and team retrospective
- Weekly delivery check-ins with PM and product leads
- Escalation updates for cross-team dependency issues

---

## Technical Lead / Architect

### Role Summary
The Technical Lead or Architect defines the technical direction for the project and ensures implementation choices support maintainability, scalability, and delivery goals. They bridge product intent with technical feasibility and help the team avoid design drift.

### Responsibilities
- Define technical architecture and design patterns
- Conduct design reviews and make technical trade-off decisions
- Identify technical risks and propose mitigations
- Mentor developers on best practices and maintainability
- Ensure technical decisions align with quality and delivery goals
- Partner with Security Lead on secure design patterns

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Product Manager**: Align technical feasibility with roadmap and customer value
- **Project Manager**: Surface risks, dependencies, and technical blockers early
- **Developers**: Provide design guidance, review code, and unblock technical decisions
- **QA**: Ensure the test strategy matches architectural risks and critical paths
- **Security Lead**: Coordinate secure design and technical risk controls

### Goals
- Maintain a clear and sustainable technical architecture
- Reduce avoidable engineering complexity
- Support delivery with strong technical standards

### Typical Communication
- Architecture review meetings and design discussions
- Code review comments and technical guidance
- Risk updates during planning and weekly delivery reviews

---

## UX / Design Lead

### Role Summary
The UX / Design Lead ensures that product experiences are usable, cohesive, and aligned to customer needs. They shape interaction design, usability, and the overall experience quality of the solution.

### Responsibilities
- Define user experience goals and design direction
- Translate customer needs into interaction and interface requirements
- Review product flows for usability and consistency
- Partner with Product Managers on prioritization and problem framing
- Ensure accessibility, clarity, and user-centered decision-making

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Product Manager**: Align on customer needs, value proposition, and feature priorities
- **Developers**: Clarify UX requirements, flows, and edge cases
- **QA**: Validate usability and acceptance against user workflows
- **Project Manager**: Coordinate design milestones and dependency tracking
- **Stakeholders**: Communicate user experience impact and trade-offs

### Goals
- Create intuitive, accessible, and high-value user experiences
- Reduce friction in customer workflows
- Ensure product design supports business outcomes

### Typical Communication
- Discovery and design reviews
- Product feedback loops and usability discussions
- Cross-functional planning and acceptance review sessions

---

## Security Lead

### Role Summary
The Security Lead ensures security requirements are built into project planning, implementation, and release processes. They help reduce risk across the product lifecycle and support secure-by-default delivery.

### Responsibilities
- Define security requirements and compliance needs
- Conduct security reviews and threat assessments
- Review designs and code for security vulnerabilities
- Define security acceptance criteria and testing approach
- Escalate security risks and recommend mitigations
- Support incident response and post-incident reviews

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Product Manager**: Align on security requirements and business risk
- **Technical Lead**: Review technical designs for vulnerabilities and secure patterns
- **Developers**: Provide guidance on secure implementation and code review
- **QA**: Define test scenarios for security validation and risk coverage
- **Project Manager**: Coordinate escalation and mitigation timelines

### Goals
- Reduce project security risk
- Ensure compliance and secure operating practices
- Improve readiness for release and incident response

### Typical Communication
- Security planning and design review sessions
- Code review activities for sensitive changes
- Security risk reporting in project updates and incident response workflows

---

## Data / Analytics Owner

### Role Summary
The Data / Analytics Owner ensures the team measures outcomes, tracks key signals, and uses evidence to improve decisions. They help define instrumentation, dashboards, and success metrics for product delivery.

### Responsibilities
- Define success metrics and analytics needs
- Ensure instrumentation and reporting are in place
- Monitor product usage, quality trends, and adoption data
- Share insights that guide product and delivery decisions
- Partner with Product and PM leads on outcome measurement

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Product Manager**: Define measurable success criteria and reporting goals
- **Project Manager**: Connect delivery progress to milestone and impact tracking
- **Developers**: Ensure instrumentation and analytics hooks are implemented correctly
- **QA**: Validate measurement flows and release impact assumptions
- **Stakeholders**: Communicate data-informed outcomes and trends

### Goals
- Improve decision-making with measurable evidence
- Track the impact of released work
- Reduce guesswork in prioritization and performance evaluation

### Typical Communication
- Weekly delivery and metric reviews
- Dashboard reporting and stakeholder updates
- Release impact and KPI evaluation sessions

---

## Stakeholder / Sponsor

### Role Summary
The Stakeholder or Sponsor provides strategic direction, approves major decisions, and ensures the project remains aligned with business goals and available resources. They help the team navigate trade-offs and executive-level concerns.

### Responsibilities
- Define strategic goals and expected business outcomes
- Approve scope, milestones, and go/no-go decisions
- Remove cross-functional or executive blockers when needed
- Review progress against business value and strategic priorities
- Support alignment across dependent teams and leadership

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Product Manager**: Align on business priorities and product outcomes
- **Project Manager**: Review schedules, risks, and stakeholder communication
- **Technical Lead**: Support architecture and sequencing decisions when trade-offs are needed
- **Developers and QA**: Provide clarity on delivery constraints and acceptance
- **Other stakeholders**: Coordinate sponsorship and cross-functional decision-making

### Goals
- Keep the project aligned to business outcomes
- Support successful delivery with strategic clarity
- Remove obstacles that prevent execution

### Typical Communication
- Milestone reviews and governance meetings
- Steering updates and executive briefings
- Decision support for blockers, scope changes, and approvals

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- These expanded roles are intended to clarify accountability, improve handoffs, and strengthen collaboration across the project lifecycle.
